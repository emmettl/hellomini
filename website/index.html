A DESKTOP WITH QUESTIONABLE PRIORITIES

Some useful.
Some utterly unnecessary.

Watch your machine work. Keep a thought. Feed a fish.
There is, technically, a productivity angle.

A tiny window on a big machine.

Activity Monitor keeps an eye on CPU, memory, and processes. Print Monitor turns GitHub and GitLab builds into a queue of imaginary print jobs.

A failed build is a paper jam. Obviously.

A place for the little things.

Keep notes, pictures, links, and commands in Scrapbook. Check a time zone, convert a unit, browse a folder, or work out where all that disk space went.

Desk accessories, with rather fewer limitations.

Entirely too much computer.

A spinning Metal teapot. A fish tank. Flying Toasters. A puzzle made from the live desktop. Modern graphics, dressed for a different decade.

Optional silliness. Impeccably poor priorities.
